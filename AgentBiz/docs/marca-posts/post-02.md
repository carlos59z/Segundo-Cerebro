Building my own automation systems taught me what most small businesses
get wrong about AI agents. Three myths, honestly:

**Myth 1: "AI replaces my team."**
It replaces the copy-paste, not the person. My systems still need a human
for every real decision — the automation just makes sure the human only
spends time on decisions, not on moving data between tables.

**Myth 2: "The agent just… works."**
No. An agent is only as good as the info you feed it. Feeding it wrong
hours or a wrong price list is worse than no agent at all. That's why
every build I do starts with a controlled test before it ever talks to a
real customer.

**Myth 3: "It will magically increase sales."**
I won't promise that, and you should distrust anyone who does. What honest
automation gives you is simpler: nothing left unanswered, no follow-up
forgotten, hours back in your week. What you do with those hours is your
business — not mine.
